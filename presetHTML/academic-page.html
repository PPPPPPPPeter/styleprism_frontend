About

I study how communities in medieval England were shaped by parish networks, land tenure, and ecclesiastical power. My work combines traditional archival research with digital humanities methods — GIS mapping, network analysis, and digital palaeography — to recover marginalised voices from monastic and parish records. I am advised by Prof. Eleanor Ashworth at the Centre for Medieval Studies.
